How to read this page

Use the same video, browser or device filter to investigate a viewing experience. Startup excludes attempts without a measurement. Buffering is weighted by duration and excludes startup wait, pauses and seeking. Missing delivery can undercount observed errors.

What “reached end” means

The final event reports that playback reached the end of the video. A viewer may have skipped ahead, so this does not prove they watched the whole video.

Average watch time includes failed, ongoing and start-only attempts. Missing final events can reduce both watch time and observed ends.

About these metrics

Views are playback attempts, including failed attempts. Unique viewers count supplied IDs, not verified people; anonymous viewers do not add an ID. All aggregate filters select the retained view start, from inclusive to exclusive. Whole cumulative playing time is assigned to that start, including playback after the window. Snapshots are never added together. Events use their own occurrence time and context for filtering. Retention, deletion and missing delivery can change the results. Do not add unique viewers across days or videos.
