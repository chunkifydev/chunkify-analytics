Try this flow

  1. Paste your video URL, start a view and let it play for at least five seconds.
  2. Open Player test analytics, then press Refresh to see events and watch time.
  3. Pause, seek or switch quality to produce more playback events.
  4. Press Finish view and refresh again to inspect the final view summary.

Snapshots report cumulative playing time every five seconds. The dashboard refreshes only when you ask it to.
